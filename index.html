I'm an ECE student at NSUT, and I like building fun things, then taking them apart to see how they work. That curiosity has taken me from wiring control panels at BHEL to cutting a 30B-parameter AI model down until it runs on a phone.

Most of my building happens on a screen: ML and data pipelines at FuturixAI, product flows, pitch decks, and 38 social posts that make technical ideas easy to follow.

Away from the screen, I'm still making things. I paint, fold origami, and will happily lose an afternoon to any kind of art. And I'm at my best with a dog around, ideally my golden retriever, who supervises most of it.

01 · Machine learning

Cutting a 30B model down until it runs on a phone.

Qwen3-30B is a mixture of experts, and one subject only needs some of them. We built a gradient-free way to turn it into subject specialists.

B.Tech team project
Illustration, not real weights
See the results ↗
Accuracy40+ points

ahead of Fisher-based structured pruning (EMNLP 2026) on GSM8K and MATH 500, at matched sparsity.

On a phone8 tokens/s

for the 30B maths specialist on a 16 GB RAM phone setup. We keep only the subject's experts and quantise the rest.

Speed to build20 minutes

per subject expert instead of 4 hours, about 12x faster than QLoRA, for a 1-point accuracy loss. It calibrates on the model's own verified solutions.

02 · Product design

Product design · Two versions

What broke

V1 opened on a timeline of everything that had happened to a file. The feedback: complicated and hard to navigate. Renaming buttons wouldn't fix that.

The rebuild

I cut the scope to editable documents, mapped owners and contributors on a whiteboard, and rebuilt the flow so the document comes first.

What I learned
