01 / Who I am

An ECE student who learns by taking things apart.

In summer 2025 I was at BHEL in Bengaluru, working on power plant automation. I helped wire, test and commission control panels, learned to read schematics and assembly drawings, and saw how a panel is proven before it goes live.

Most of my building happens on a screen. At FuturixAI I built a document pipeline across 64+ government and procurement sources, then checked what LLMs pulled out of those documents against reference records. At NSUT I'm on a team cutting a 30B-parameter model down into subject specialists that run on a phone.

And I design: product flows, pitch decks, and 38 social posts that turn technical ideas into something people stop for. Whatever I work on, I want to know how it actually works, then explain it so anyone can follow.

03 / What I want to build next

A calculator that shows its working.

Our maths specialist already runs at 8 tokens a second on a phone setup. Next, I want to put it inside something you can hold: a pocket device with a keypad and a small screen that solves a maths problem, explains every step, and never needs the internet.

The brain

The maths specialist, already running on-device in 16 GB of RAM. A 16 GB single-board computer is the next stop.

The body

A keypad, a screen, a battery, and a 3D-printed case that fits in a pocket.

The story

Filmed from first boot to first right answer, including everything that breaks on the way.

What's stopping me

A workbench, parts, and people who build physical things every day. Builders.mu has all three.
